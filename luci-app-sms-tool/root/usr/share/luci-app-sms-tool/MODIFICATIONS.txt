Changes add unified and standalone SMS menus, QDC507 port selection,
multipart grouping, a separate collection service, durable PDU archives,
verified per-slot buffer cleanup, browser polling and local-record deletion.
Sending, USSD, AT and reception share per-port locks; user input is quoted.
Sample contacts and example device identifiers are removed from defaults.

Original PDUs and decoded messages are saved and read back before SIM
identity and modem slot contents are verified for cleanup. The browser
reads local history and uses CSRF-protected POST for selected local records.
The separate sms-tool C executable retains Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause and
HTML Tidy attribution and licenses under /usr/share/sms-tool/.
